# Chips & Cookies

Cookie and chip ordering with owner-approved delivery in Texas from Forney.

## Language

**Order**:
A customer's products and quantities for delivery on a chosen date. Owners can
revise cookie quantities after payment until baking begins; increases require
additional payment and reductions receive partial refunds.
_Avoid_: Payment, transaction

**Outstanding balance**:
The amount still owed on an order, including unpaid additions. An owner may
authorize delivery before this balance is paid for a trusted customer.
_Avoid_: Unpaid order

**Delivery area**:
Texas addresses eligible for owner review. Every delivery requires approval;
there is no fixed one-hour driving limit.
_Avoid_: Shipping region

**Delivery fee**:
The owner-approved charge for bringing an order to the customer's address,
based on travel distance, driving time, and delivery costs without a subsidy.
_Avoid_: Shipping fee

**Delivery lead time**:
The advance notice required for a customer's chosen delivery date: at least one
week, with the chosen date subject to owner approval.
_Avoid_: Shipping time
